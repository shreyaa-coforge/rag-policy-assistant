# Helix Dynamics Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy Owner:** Finance
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees of Helix Dynamics in the United States

## EXP-1 Purpose and Scope

This policy explains which business expenses Helix Dynamics will reimburse, the limits that apply, and how employees should submit expenses for repayment. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees who spend their own money, or use a company corporate card, on approved company business. Contractors should follow the expense terms in their individual contracts, and interns should follow this policy unless their offer letter says otherwise.

The goal of this policy is to make reimbursement fair, fast, and consistent across every team. It also helps the company meet its tax and audit obligations. Where this policy sets a limit, the limit is a maximum, not a target. Employees are expected to spend less when a reasonable lower-cost option is available.

## EXP-2 General Principles

### EXP-2.1 Reasonable and Necessary

Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own. An expense is reimbursable only if it is reasonable in amount, necessary for business, and supported by proper documentation. When two options would meet the same business need, employees should choose the lower-cost option unless there is a clear safety, time, or accessibility reason not to. Managers may reject any expense that does not meet these standards, even if it falls within a stated limit.

### EXP-2.2 Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Spends within policy limits, keeps receipts, and submits accurate expense reports on time |
| Manager | Reviews each report for business purpose and policy compliance before approving |
| Department Head | Approves higher-value reports and any exceptions for their department |
| Finance | Processes payments, runs audits, answers policy questions, and maintains this policy |
| Travel Desk | Supports trip booking, changes, and travel emergencies through Navan |

### EXP-2.3 Exceptions

Any expense that falls outside this policy requires written pre-approval from the department head. Exceptions should be requested by email before the expense is incurred, and the approval email must be attached to the expense report when it is submitted. Exceptions approved after the fact are rare and are reviewed individually by Finance. Approval of one exception does not create a standing exception for future expenses.

## EXP-3 Travel

### EXP-3.1 Airfare

Employees must book flights through the company travel portal, Navan, at least 14 days before departure when possible. Fares booked fewer than 7 days before departure must include a short explanation in the expense report. The approved class of service depends on the length of the flight:

| Flight Duration (one way) | Approved Class of Service |
|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 hours or longer | Premium economy |
| Any duration, with CFO pre-approval | Business |

Checked bag fees for one standard bag per trip are reimbursable. Seat selection fees are reimbursable only for flights longer than 3 hours. Airline club memberships, in-flight Wi-Fi for personal use, and fees for changing a flight for personal reasons are not reimbursable. Employees may keep frequent flyer miles earned on business trips, but may not choose a more expensive flight to earn them.

### EXP-3.2 Lodging

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to the nightly limits below, before taxes and mandatory fees. Employees should book through Navan to receive negotiated company rates.

| City Tier | Cities Included | Nightly Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | All U.S. cities not listed as high-cost | $250 |
| High-cost | New York, San Francisco, Boston, Seattle, Washington, D.C. | $325 |
| International | All cities outside the United States | $300 |

If the official conference hotel costs more than the limit, employees may book the conference hotel with manager approval. Short-term rentals, such as apartments booked through a rental site, are allowed for stays of 5 nights or more if the total cost is lower than a comparable hotel. Room service, minibar charges, and in-room movies are not reimbursable. Laundry service is reimbursable only for trips longer than 5 consecutive nights.

### EXP-3.3 Ground Transportation and Mileage

Rideshare, taxis, trains, airport shuttles, and standard rental cars are reimbursable when used for business travel. Employees should choose a standard or compact rental car unless they are traveling with 3 or more colleagues or carrying large equipment. Fuel for rental cars is reimbursable; prepaid fuel options offered by the rental company are not.

Employees using a personal vehicle for business travel are reimbursed at **$0.70 per mile**. Mileage must be logged with the date, starting point, destination, and business purpose. Normal commuting between home and the employee's assigned office is not reimbursable. Parking at the airport is reimbursable up to the cost of a round-trip rideshare to the same airport. Tolls are reimbursable when a receipt or toll statement is provided.

### EXP-3.4 Booking a Business Trip

Employees should follow these steps for every overnight business trip:

1. Confirm the business purpose and dates of the trip with your manager.
2. Request travel approval in Navan at least 14 days before departure.
3. Book flights, hotels, and rental cars through Navan once approval is received.
4. Save every receipt and confirmation email during the trip.
5. Log any personal vehicle mileage on the day it is driven.
6. Submit all trip expenses together in one expense report after you return.

Trips booked outside Navan may still be reimbursed, but the employee must show that the cost was equal to or lower than the Navan price at the time of booking. If travel plans change because of weather, illness, or a flight cancellation, employees should contact the Travel Desk, which is available 24 hours a day.

## EXP-4 Meals

### EXP-4.1 Client Meals

Meals with clients, candidates, or business partners are reimbursed up to **$100 per person**, including tax and tip. The expense report must list every attendee, their company, and the business purpose of the meal. The most senior Helix Dynamics employee present should pay for the meal and submit the expense. Alcohol is reimbursable at client meals only, up to 2 drinks per person. Meals with government officials require Legal approval in advance.

### EXP-4.2 Daily Meal Allowance

When traveling overnight for business, employees may claim up to **$75 per day** for their own meals, including tax and tip. The daily allowance is divided by meal as shown below:

| Meal | Daily Limit |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | $15 |
| Lunch | $20 |
| Dinner | $40 |
| **Total per day** | **$75** |

On the first and last day of a trip, the allowance is 75% of the daily amount. Meals already provided by a conference, hotel, or airline may not be claimed. Unused amounts from one meal may be applied to another meal on the same day, but may not be carried to another day. Alcohol is not covered under the daily meal allowance. Same-day trips without an overnight stay do not qualify for the daily meal allowance.

### EXP-4.3 Team Meals

Team celebration meals, such as a project launch dinner, require pre-approval from a director and are capped at **$60 per person**, including tax and tip. Team meals are limited to 2 per team per quarter. Regular working lunches with coworkers are not reimbursable unless they take place during an approved offsite or a meeting that runs through a normal meal time.

## EXP-5 Receipts and Documentation

Itemized receipts are required for any single expense of **$40 or more**. Credit card statements and payment confirmations without item details are not accepted as receipts. Digital photos of paper receipts are accepted if all details are readable. Documentation requirements by expense type are listed below:

| Expense Type | Documentation Required |
|---|---|
| Airfare | Itinerary showing class of service and fare paid |
| Lodging | Itemized hotel folio showing the nightly rate and any extra charges |
| Client and team meals | Itemized receipt with attendees and business purpose listed |
| Mileage | Mileage log with dates, locations, distance, and business purpose |
| Conference fees | Registration confirmation and agenda |
| Other expenses | Itemized receipt and business purpose |

If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete **Form FIN-118 (Missing Receipt Declaration)**, which a manager must sign. Employees may submit no more than **3 missing receipt declarations per calendar year**. Additional missing receipts will not be reimbursed.

## EXP-6 Submitting Expenses

### EXP-6.1 Submission Deadline

All expenses must be submitted in Concur within **30 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days require written approval from the department head, and expenses submitted after **90 days** will not be reimbursed. Approved reimbursements are paid by direct deposit within **10 business days** of final approval. Employees can track the status of each report in Concur.

### EXP-6.2 Expense Codes

Each expense must be coded so it routes to the correct cost center:

| Expense Code | Category |
|---|---|
| T&E-07 | Travel and entertainment |
| TRN-02 | Training, courses, and certifications |
| SUB-04 | Software and subscriptions |
| OFF-01 | Office supplies |
| CON-05 | Conference registration fees |

Using the wrong code may delay payment. Finance may recode an expense during review and will notify the employee if it does. Expenses that do not fit any code should be submitted with a note, and Finance will assign the correct code.

## EXP-7 Approval Process

Every expense report is reviewed by the employee's manager. Higher-value reports require additional approval according to the matrix below:

| Report Total | Required Approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,500 | Manager |
| $2,500.01 to $10,000 | Manager and department head |
| Over $10,000 | Manager, department head, and VP of Finance |

Approvers should act on reports within 5 business days. If an approver is out of the office, the report is automatically routed to their delegate in Concur. Employees may not approve their own expenses or the expenses of their own manager. Reports returned for corrections must be resubmitted within 10 business days.

## EXP-8 Corporate Cards

### EXP-8.1 Eligibility

Employees who travel at least 4 times a year, or who regularly purchase business supplies, may request a Helix Dynamics corporate card. Requests are made through the Finance portal and require manager approval. Cards are issued in the employee's name and remain company property.

### EXP-8.2 Using the Card

The corporate card may be used only for business expenses that follow this policy. Personal charges are not allowed, even if the employee plans to repay them. Cash withdrawals are limited to $200 per trip and only when a vendor does not accept cards. Lost or stolen cards must be reported to the card issuer and to Finance within 24 hours.

### EXP-8.3 Reconciling Card Charges

Card charges appear in Concur automatically. Employees must attach receipts and submit each charge within the same deadlines as personal expenses. Cards with charges more than 60 days old that have not been reconciled will be suspended until the charges are submitted. Repeated suspensions may lead to the card being cancelled.

## EXP-9 Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:

- Personal entertainment, such as movies, sporting events, or spa services
- Traffic tickets, parking fines, or towing charges
- Upgrades to seats, rooms, or rental cars not approved in advance
- Expenses for family members or guests who are not business attendees
- Annual fees for personal credit cards or loyalty programs
- Gifts to coworkers, including holiday and birthday gifts
- Lost or stolen personal property
- Childcare or pet care while traveling
- Political or charitable contributions made in the company's name

## EXP-10 Audits and Policy Violations

Finance audits a random sample of expense reports each month and may ask for more documentation at any time. Employees must respond to audit requests within 10 business days. Submitting false, duplicate, or inflated expenses is a serious violation and may lead to repayment, loss of corporate card privileges, and disciplinary action up to and including termination.
