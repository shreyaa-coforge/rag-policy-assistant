# Helix Dynamics Remote Work Policy

**Policy Owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees of Helix Dynamics in the United States

## RW-1 Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how Helix Dynamics employees can work from locations other than a company office. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees in the United States. Contractors and interns follow the remote work terms in their individual agreements. Employees outside the United States should follow the remote work guidelines for their country.

The goal of this policy is to give employees flexibility in where they work while keeping teams connected, productive, and secure. Remote work is a business arrangement, not an employee benefit or entitlement, and it may be adjusted when business needs change.

## RW-2 Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Assigned office | The Helix Dynamics office listed as the employee's work location in Workday |
| Hybrid employee | An employee who splits the work week between the assigned office and an approved remote location |
| Fully remote employee | An employee approved to work from a remote location without a regular office schedule |
| Anchor day | A day each week when a whole team is expected to work on site |
| Remote work agreement | The approved Form HR-210 that records an employee's remote arrangement |
| Approved remote location | The home address or other location listed in the employee's remote work agreement |

## RW-3 Eligibility

### RW-3.1 Eligible Employees

Most roles at Helix Dynamics are eligible for hybrid remote work. An employee becomes eligible after completing their first **60 days** of employment and receiving a "meets expectations" rating or higher on their most recent performance review. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible for new remote arrangements until the plan is completed.

### RW-3.2 Eligibility by Job Family

Managers decide eligibility for each role, and People Operations keeps the official list of eligible job families. The general rules are shown below:

| Job Family | Hybrid Eligible | Fully Remote Eligible |
|---|---|---|
| Software Engineering | Yes | Yes, with approval |
| Product and Design | Yes | Yes, with approval |
| Sales and Account Management | Yes | Yes, with approval |
| Finance and People Operations | Yes | No |
| Laboratory and Research Operations | No | No |
| Facilities and Warehouse | No | No |
| On-site Customer Support | No | No |

Roles that require hands-on work with lab equipment, physical inventory, or on-site customers are not eligible for regular remote work. These employees may still request occasional remote days for training or administrative work with manager approval.

## RW-4 Work Arrangements

### RW-4.1 Types of Arrangements

| Arrangement | Minimum Office Days | Approval Needed |
|---|---|---|
| On-site | 5 days per week | None |
| Hybrid | 2 days per week | Manager |
| Fully remote | One team gathering per quarter | Department head and VP of People Operations |
| Temporary remote | Set case by case | Manager and People Operations |

### RW-4.2 Hybrid Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Employees must spend at least 2 days per week in their assigned office. Each team sets one shared anchor day when everyone is expected on site, and that anchor day counts toward the 2 required office days. Hybrid employees should agree on their regular office days with their manager and record them in Workday so that desk booking and building access are planned correctly.

### RW-4.3 Fully Remote Arrangements

Fully remote arrangements are available only with written approval from both the employee's department head and the VP of People Operations. Fully remote employees must attend one in-person team gathering per quarter. Helix Dynamics pays travel costs for these gatherings under the Expense Reimbursement Policy. A fully remote employee who later moves within commuting distance of an office may be asked to change to a hybrid arrangement.

### RW-4.4 Temporary Remote Arrangements

Employees who are not normally eligible may request a temporary remote arrangement for up to 8 weeks in special situations, such as recovery from an injury, a family care need, or an office closure. Temporary arrangements are approved by the manager and People Operations and end automatically on the date listed in the approval.

## RW-5 Requesting Remote Work

### RW-5.1 How to Submit a Request

To start a hybrid, fully remote, or temporary remote arrangement, employees should follow these steps:

1. Discuss the proposed arrangement and schedule with your manager.
2. Complete **Form HR-210 (Remote Work Agreement)** in Workday.
3. Enter your approved remote location and your proposed office days.
4. Complete the home workspace safety checklist attached to the form.
5. Submit the form at least **10 business days** before the proposed start date.

### RW-5.2 Review Timeline

| Request Type | Reviewed By | Decision Within |
|---|---|---|
| Hybrid | Manager | 5 business days |
| Fully remote | Department head, then VP of People Operations | 15 business days |
| Temporary remote | Manager and People Operations | 3 business days |

Approved agreements are reviewed once a year, or sooner if the employee changes roles, managers, or home address. Employees whose requests are denied may ask People Operations for a second review.

## RW-6 Equipment and Stipends

### RW-6.1 Company Equipment

Helix Dynamics provides standard equipment to every remote-eligible employee:

| Item | Provided To | Replacement Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop | All employees | Every 3 years |
| External monitor | Hybrid and fully remote employees | Every 5 years |
| Headset | Hybrid and fully remote employees | Every 2 years |
| Keyboard and mouse | Hybrid and fully remote employees | As needed |
| Mobile phone | Roles that require on-call support | Every 3 years |

Requests for additional equipment, such as a second monitor or an ergonomic chair for a medical need, should be sent to the IT Service Desk. All company equipment remains company property.

### RW-6.2 Home Office Stipend

Employees with an approved remote work agreement receive a one-time **home office stipend of $500** for items such as a desk, chair, or lighting. Employees also receive a monthly **internet stipend of $40**, paid through payroll. Stipend purchases do not require receipts, but the stipend is treated as taxable income. Employees who leave within 6 months of receiving the home office stipend are not required to repay it.

### RW-6.3 Returning Equipment

All company equipment must be returned within **5 business days** of an employee's last day of work. The IT Service Desk will send a prepaid shipping box to remote employees. Equipment that is not returned may be deducted from the final paycheck where allowed by law.

## RW-7 Information Security

### RW-7.1 Network and Devices

Remote employees must follow all Information Security policies. In particular, employees must:

- Always connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN turned on.
- Lock their screen whenever they step away from their device.
- Keep their laptop's operating system and security software up to date.
- Use only company-approved tools for storing and sharing files.

### RW-7.2 Data Handling at Remote Locations

| Data Classification | Example | Allowed at Remote Location |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Published marketing materials | Yes |
| Internal | Team meeting notes, internal wikis | Yes, on company devices only |
| Confidential | Financial reports, employee records | Yes, on company devices with VPN connected |
| Restricted | Customer data, source code signing keys | Only with written approval from Information Security |

Customer data may never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Confidential documents may not be printed at home unless the employee's manager approves it and the printed copies are shredded after use.

### RW-7.3 Reporting Security Incidents

Lost or stolen equipment, suspected phishing, or any unusual account activity must be reported to the IT Service Desk within **24 hours**. Employees should not try to investigate a security incident on their own.

## RW-8 Availability and Communication

### RW-8.1 Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable during **core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time**, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they meet their team's commitments. Employees should keep their calendar and chat status up to date so teammates know when they are available.

### RW-8.2 Response Time Expectations

| Channel | Expected Response During Core Hours |
|---|---|
| Phone call or urgent page | Within 15 minutes |
| Team chat message | Within 1 hour |
| Email | Within 1 business day |
| Calendar invitation | Within 2 business days |

### RW-8.3 Meetings

Cameras should be on for team meetings unless there is a connection issue. Meetings that include both office and remote employees should use a meeting room with video equipment so that everyone can participate equally.

### RW-8.4 Working Across Time Zones

Fully remote employees who live outside the Eastern Time zone must still be available during core hours, adjusted to their local time. For example, an employee in the Pacific Time zone must be available from 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM local time. Teams that span several time zones should rotate recurring meeting times each quarter so that the same people are not always meeting early or late.

## RW-9 Working from Another Location

### RW-9.1 Domestic Travel

Employees may work temporarily from another location within the United States for up to **4 weeks per calendar year** without additional approval, as long as they tell their manager in advance.

### RW-9.2 International Remote Work

Working from outside the United States requires approval from People Operations and the Legal team because of tax and data protection rules. International remote work is limited to **30 calendar days per year**.

| Request | Approvers | Submit At Least |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic, up to 4 weeks | Manager notification only | 3 business days before |
| Domestic, more than 4 weeks | Manager and People Operations | 10 business days before |
| International, up to 30 days | People Operations and Legal | 30 days before travel |

## RW-10 Health, Safety, and Workspace

Remote employees are responsible for keeping their workspace safe and free of hazards. The workspace should have a stable desk, a supportive chair, and enough lighting. Work-related injuries that happen at an approved remote location during working hours must be reported to People Operations within **48 hours**, just as they would be in the office.

Employees may request a free virtual ergonomic assessment through People Operations once a year. If the assessment recommends equipment that is not covered by the home office stipend, such as a standing desk converter, the IT Service Desk will review the request and may provide the item as company property.

## RW-11 Performance and Changes to Arrangements

Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as office employees. Either the employee or the manager may end a remote arrangement with **2 weeks of written notice**. A manager may also end an arrangement right away if security rules are broken or if performance drops below expectations.

## RW-12 Policy Violations and Contacts

Failure to follow this policy, including the security requirements in RW-7, may lead to the remote arrangement being ended and may result in disciplinary action. Questions about this policy should be sent to People Operations at people-ops@helixdynamics.example. Equipment and security questions should be sent to the IT Service Desk at it-help@helixdynamics.example.
