# Helix Dynamics Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Policy Owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 4.1
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees of Helix Dynamics in the United States

## PTO-1 Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how employees at Helix Dynamics earn, request, and use paid time off, sick leave, company holidays, and other types of leave. Helix Dynamics believes that regular time away from work helps employees stay healthy and do their best work, and managers are expected to encourage their teams to use their time off.

### PTO-1.1 Eligibility by Employee Type

| Employee Type | PTO | Sick Leave | Paid Holidays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time (30 or more hours per week) | Full accrual | 10 days per year | Yes |
| Part-time (20 to 29 hours per week) | Half of full-time accrual | 5 days per year | Yes, prorated |
| Part-time (under 20 hours per week) | Not eligible | As required by state law | No |
| Temporary staff and contractors | Not eligible | As required by state law | No |
| Interns | Not eligible | 3 days per internship | Yes |

Where state or local law requires more generous leave than this policy, Helix Dynamics follows the law.

## PTO-2 PTO Accrual

### PTO-2.1 Accrual Rates

PTO accrues each pay period based on years of service. Helix Dynamics pays employees twice a month, for 24 pay periods each year.

| Years of Service | Annual PTO | Accrued Per Pay Period |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 years | 15 days | 5 hours |
| 3 to 5 years | 20 days | 6.67 hours |
| 6 or more years | 25 days | 8.33 hours |

Years of service are counted from the employee's original hire date. Employees move to the next accrual rate at the start of the pay period after their work anniversary.

### PTO-2.2 Waiting Period

New employees start accruing PTO on their first day of work. However, PTO may not be used until the employee has completed **90 days** of employment, except in cases approved by People Operations, such as a family emergency or a pre-planned event disclosed before the start date.

### PTO-2.3 Accrual Cap

Employees may hold a maximum balance of **1.5 times their annual PTO amount**. Once an employee reaches this cap, they stop accruing new PTO until their balance drops below the cap. For example, an employee who earns 15 days per year will stop accruing when their balance reaches 22.5 days. Workday sends a reminder when an employee's balance reaches 90% of the cap.

## PTO-3 Carryover

### PTO-3.1 Year-End Carryover

At the end of each calendar year, employees may carry over up to **5 days** of unused PTO into the next year. Any unused PTO above 5 days is forfeited on December 31. Carried-over days must be used by **March 31** of the following year, or they will expire.

### PTO-3.2 Carryover Examples

| Unused PTO on December 31 | Days Carried Over | Days Forfeited | Use Carried Days By |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 days | 3 days | 0 days | March 31 |
| 5 days | 5 days | 0 days | March 31 |
| 9 days | 5 days | 4 days | March 31 |

Carried-over days are always used before newly accrued days when PTO is taken.

### PTO-3.3 Exceptions

If a documented business need prevents an employee from using their PTO before the end of the year, the department head may request a one-time carryover exception from People Operations. Exceptions must be requested before **December 1** and may allow up to 5 additional days to be carried over. Exceptions are not granted for PTO that was simply not scheduled.

## PTO-4 Requesting PTO

### PTO-4.1 Notice Requirements

All PTO requests must be submitted through Workday. Employees should give notice based on the length of the request:

| Length of Request | Minimum Notice | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 1 day | 1 business day | Manager |
| 1 to 2 days | 3 business days | Manager |
| 3 or more consecutive days | 2 weeks | Manager |
| More than 10 consecutive days | 30 days | Manager and department head |

### PTO-4.2 Approval

Managers should respond to requests within **3 business days**. Requests are generally approved in the order they are received. When several team members request the same dates, managers should consider coverage needs first and then the order in which requests were submitted. Approved PTO should not be cancelled by a manager except in a true business emergency, and the company will reimburse any non-refundable travel costs the employee loses as a result.

### PTO-4.3 Blackout Periods

Some teams have busy periods when PTO requests may be limited. PTO during these periods requires department head approval.

| Team | Blackout Period |
|---|---|
| Finance | Last 5 business days of each quarter and the first 10 business days of January |
| Sales | Last 2 weeks of each quarter |
| Customer Support | The week of any major product launch |
| All teams | Company-wide planning week in early October |

### PTO-4.4 Changing or Cancelling Approved PTO

Employees may cancel or shorten approved PTO in Workday at any time before the time off begins, and the unused hours are returned to their balance. Changing the dates of approved PTO counts as a new request and must meet the notice requirements in the table above. If an employee becomes ill during approved PTO for 2 or more consecutive days, those days may be changed to sick leave by contacting People Operations within 5 business days of returning to work.

## PTO-5 Company Holidays

Helix Dynamics observes **11 paid holidays** each year. Holidays do not count against an employee's PTO balance.

| Holiday | Typical Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | January 1 |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | Third Monday in January |
| Presidents' Day | Third Monday in February |
| Memorial Day | Last Monday in May |
| Juneteenth | June 19 |
| Independence Day | July 4 |
| Labor Day | First Monday in September |
| Thanksgiving Day | Fourth Thursday in November |
| Day after Thanksgiving | Fourth Friday in November |
| Christmas Eve | December 24 |
| Christmas Day | December 25 |

If a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the Friday before. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the Monday after. Employees who are required to work on a holiday receive a substitute day off to be used within 60 days.

## PTO-6 Sick Leave

### PTO-6.1 Sick Leave Allowance

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO. Full-time employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave** each calendar year, credited on January 1. New employees receive a prorated amount based on their start date. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out at separation.

### PTO-6.2 Permitted Uses

Sick leave may be used for:

- The employee's own illness, injury, or medical appointments
- Caring for an ill family member, including a spouse, partner, child, or parent
- Preventive care, such as annual checkups and vaccinations
- Mental health days

### PTO-6.3 Notice and Documentation

Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on any day they are taking sick leave, and before the start of their scheduled shift when they can. A doctor's note is required for absences of **more than 3 consecutive workdays**. Notes should be sent to People Operations, not to the manager, to protect employee privacy.

### PTO-6.4 Extended Illness

Employees who need more than 10 consecutive workdays away because of a serious health condition should contact People Operations as soon as possible. After sick leave is used, the employee may use available PTO and may be eligible for short-term disability benefits or protected leave under federal and state law. People Operations will explain the options and help the employee complete the required forms.

## PTO-7 Other Types of Leave

### PTO-7.1 Bereavement Leave

| Relationship | Paid Bereavement Days |
|---|---|
| Spouse, partner, child, or parent | 5 days |
| Sibling, grandparent, or grandchild | 5 days |
| Other relatives, including in-laws, aunts, uncles, and cousins | 2 days |

Bereavement days do not need to be taken consecutively but should be used within 60 days of the loss.

### PTO-7.2 Jury Duty

Employees called for jury duty receive their regular pay for up to **10 working days** per year. A copy of the jury summons must be submitted to People Operations. Employees released from jury duty early in the day are not expected to return to work that day.

### PTO-7.3 Volunteer Leave

Full-time employees receive **2 paid volunteer days** each calendar year to support registered nonprofit organizations. Volunteer days must be requested in Workday with the same notice as PTO.

### PTO-7.4 Unpaid Leave

Employees who have used all available PTO may request unpaid leave of up to **30 days**. Unpaid leave requires approval from the department head and People Operations. Health benefits continue during approved unpaid leave.

### PTO-7.5 Parental Leave

Employees who have completed **6 months** of employment are eligible for paid parental leave after the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

| Parent Type | Paid Parental Leave |
|---|---|
| Birth parent | 16 weeks |
| Non-birth parent | 8 weeks |
| Adoptive or foster parent | 8 weeks |

Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be taken all at once or in up to 2 separate blocks of at least 2 weeks each. Employees should notify their manager and People Operations at least 30 days before leave begins when possible. PTO continues to accrue during paid parental leave.

### PTO-7.6 Leave Summary

| Leave Type | Paid | Annual Amount | Carries Over |
|---|---|---|---|
| PTO | Yes | 15 to 25 days | Up to 5 days |
| Sick leave | Yes | 10 days | No |
| Bereavement | Yes | 2 to 5 days per loss | Not applicable |
| Jury duty | Yes | Up to 10 working days | No |
| Volunteer days | Yes | 2 days | No |
| Parental leave | Yes | 8 to 16 weeks per event | Not applicable |
| Unpaid leave | No | Up to 30 days | Not applicable |

## PTO-8 Recording and Tracking Time Off

All PTO, sick leave, and other leave must be recorded in Workday, even for partial days. Partial days are recorded in hours. Employees can view their current balances, accrual history, and upcoming approved time off in Workday at any time. Managers receive a monthly report of team balances to help plan coverage and encourage employees to take time off.

## PTO-9 PTO at Separation

When an employee leaves Helix Dynamics, any accrued and unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck, up to a maximum of the employee's annual PTO amount. Carried-over days from the prior year are included in this calculation. Sick leave, volunteer days, and substitute holidays are not paid out. Employees who leave during their first 90 days are paid for accrued PTO according to state law.
